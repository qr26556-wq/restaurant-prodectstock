MULTI-BRANCH MULTI-DEVICE

Plans:
1. Multi-branch — 30 days
2. Multi-branch — Lifetime

Activation:
- Owner generates ONE activation code.
- Customer redeems it once in Settings.
- The plan is saved in Firebase under the restaurant's main settings.
- It is not tied to one phone.

Devices:
- Same authorized Google/Firebase account can sign in on multiple devices.
- Each device can select its own active branch.
- Branch selection is local to that device.
- Products, stock, orders, sales, customers and tables remain in Firebase Cloud.

Important:
- Multi-branch Lifetime never expires.
- Multi-branch 30 days stops being active after planExpiresAt.
- A different Google account needs its own authorized staff profile.
