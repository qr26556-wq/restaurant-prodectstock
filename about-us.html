About Al Hadi Store

Quality, trust and style — delivered across Pakistan.

Our Story

Al Hadi Store started in Lahore with a simple goal: make quality clothing, footwear, electronics and everyday lifestyle products easy to find and affordable for everyone in Pakistan. What began as a small local shop has grown into an online store trusted by customers across the country.

What We Sell

  • Men's & women's clothing — printed tees, cord sets, embroidered lawn and more
  • Footwear — casual slides, sandals and everyday shoes
  • Mobile accessories — wireless earbuds and everyday tech
  • Electronics & lifestyle gadgets

Why Shop With Us

  • Original products — every item is checked before it's listed.
  • Cash on Delivery — pay when your order arrives at your door.
  • Easy returns — hassle-free returns within 3 days of delivery.
  • Real customer support — reach us directly on WhatsApp or email, no call centres.

Our Promise

We personally pack and check every order before it leaves our store. If something isn't right, we make it right — that's the trust we've built our name on.
